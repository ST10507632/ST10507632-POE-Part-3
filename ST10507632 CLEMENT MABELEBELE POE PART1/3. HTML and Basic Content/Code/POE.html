Protecting Life Beneath The waves


OCEANGIFT is a nonprofit organisation dedicated to preserving ocean wildlife and restoring marine ecosystems.
We work to protect endangered species, clean coastlines, and inspire communities to take action for a healthier planet.
Every ripple of support helps safeguard the beauty and balance of our oceans the greatest gift we can give to future generations.

Vision Statement

safeguarded for generations to come
